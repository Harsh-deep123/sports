## Brand & Style

The design system projects elite athletic performance, technical precision, and modern dynamic energy. It targets athletes, fitness enthusiasts, and performance-minded consumers who value technical excellence, velocity, and enduring quality. The interface evokes discipline, kinetic readiness, and elevated clarity.

The visual direction embraces **Athletic Minimalism** paired with high-velocity accents:
- **Disciplined Canvas:** Expansive clean surfaces reduce cognitive clutter, letting technical products, dynamic sportswear textures, and engineered details lead the visual hierarchy.
- **Kinetic Energy:** Electric impulse accents slice through monochromatic layouts to command immediate action and highlight crucial status states.
- **Architectonic Structure:** Crisp geometry, structured editorial grids, and high-impact typographic pacing inspired by contemporary track-and-field and technical running apparel brands.

## Colors

The palette balances stark, high-contrast monochrome tones with vibrant athletic charge.

- **Primary (`#FF5500`):** Electric Impulse Orange. Dedicated exclusively to primary calls-to-action, active selection markers, critical conversion pathways, and dynamic interactive hover feedback. Never dilute its urgency across decorative non-interactive elements.
- **Secondary (`#111418`):** Deep Obsidian. Powers dominant typographic statements, high-contrast badges, deep technical dark sections, solid navigation bars, and structural anchors.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`):** Kinetic Emerald. Applied strictly to inventory readiness, verified fitness performance badges, positive availability indicators, and tactical discount flags.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Technical Slate. Provides balanced secondary hierarchy for product specs, technical descriptors, unselected states, and subdued metadata.
- **Canvas & Surface System:** 
  - Pure Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) forms the foundational canvas for high-definition product imagery.
  - Cool Off-White Neutrals (`#F8F9FA` to `#F1F3F5`) articulate secondary card containers, background bands, specification tables, and filters.
  - Micro-Borders (`#E2E8F0` to `#EDF2F7`) maintain structured modular discipline without visual friction.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is built on an intentional juxtaposition between high-tempo athletic power and clean ergonomics:

- **Display & Headings (`Barlow Condensed`):** Rendered with tight leading and prominent uppercase casing for hero banners, collection headers, and promotional callouts. The condensed structure evokes track lane lines, architectural sports gear framing, and high velocity.
- **Body & Product Intelligence (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers pristine legibility across product specs, descriptions, checkout forms, and order receipts. Its balanced geometry prevents eye fatigue during technical browsing.
- **Nutritional, Tech Specs, & Functional Labels (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase `Barlow Condensed` with increased tracking (`0.08em`) to enforce military-grade utility on product dimension chips, technical filter tags, and system indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a responsive 12-column fluid grid system optimized for high-density e-commerce conversion and large-scale photography.

- **Breakpoints & Layout:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, max content width 1440px, `gutter-lg` (2rem), `margin-lg` (3rem). Allows full 4-column product grids and asymmetric 8/4 editorial/e-commerce split lockups.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid, `gutter` (1.5rem), `margin` (1.5rem). Product feeds shift cleanly to 2 or 3 columns.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `gutter-sm` (1rem), `margin-sm` (1rem). Product grids collapse into a rigid 2-column or high-impact full-bleed card rail.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Micro-gaps inside components adhere strictly to the 4px baseline (`space-xs` through `space-xl`). Elements maintain tight internal cohesion with generous whitespace between distinct content tiers to project luxury and high-performance focus.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of crisp 1px mechanical containment lines and diffused low-opacity ambient shadows:

- **Baseline Level (Canvas):** Flat `#FFFFFF` and `#F8F9FA` backgrounds with zero shadow.
- **Structural Elevation (Cards & Product Panels):** Structured with a hair-thin 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) paired with an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(17, 20, 24, 0.04)`.
- **Active Elevation (Card Hover & Quick-Shop Overlays):** Elevation increases with a crisp upward translation (-3px) and an expanded athletic shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(17, 20, 24, 0.08)`, sharpening the container's 1px border to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Floating Modals, Sticky Carts, & Flyout Drawers:** High-tier elevation using deep diffused containment: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(17, 20, 24, 0.16)` over a translucent blurred backdrop (`rgba(17, 20, 24, 0.45)` with `backdrop-blur-md`).

## Shapes

The system implements a controlled **Rounded (`2`)** geometry that balances aggressive mechanical performance with ergonomic, human-centric wearability.

- Standard UI containers, cards, and input fields utilize `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) or `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for a modern, tailored silhouette.
- Key touch targets and dynamic status chips leverage full pill structures (`rounded-full`) to echo dynamic movement and rapid tactile touchpoints.
- Hero media assets and featured athlete spotlights use precise `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) corners to frame technical apparel visuals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Athletic CTA:** High-voltage `#FF5500` background, pure white bold uppercase text (`Barlow Condensed`), `rounded-lg` or pill silhouette, 0 10px 20px -5px rgba(255, 85, 0, 0.35) glow shadow. Hover transitions to `#E04B00` with -1px vertical lift.
- **Secondary Action:** Solid obsidian `#111418` fill, white text, matching height and radius. Hover transitions to `#1E232A`.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Transparent background with a 1.5px border (`#111418` or `#E2E8F0`), obsidian text, sharp transition to tinted off-white fill (`#F1F3F5`) on hover.

### Product Cards
- **Base Architecture:** Built on `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and `rounded-xl` geometry. 
- **Image Staging:** Products sit on neutral `#F8F9FA` photo beds with subtle zoom transitions on card hover.
- **Floating Meta:** Pill tags for discounts and inventory anchor cleanly at the top-left; interactive wishlist heart icons float top-right on frosted pill surfaces.
- **Footer:** Crisp typography stack with condensed titles, slate category breadcrumb, and bold obsidian pricing.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Discount & Sale Badges:** Pill-shaped tags featuring Kinetic Emerald (`#10B981`) text on `#ECFDF5` background or solid Obsidian `#111418` with white text for premium collections.
- **High-Impact Badges (`NEW`, `BESTSELLER`):** Crisp uppercase text in `label-caps` inside an electric orange or pure obsidian micro-pill.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Minimum 48px touch-height, `#F8F9FA` fill, crisp 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), `rounded-lg`. Active focus immediately outlines with 2px solid `#FF5500` with 0px outline offset for athletic precision.
- **Size & Weight Selectors:** Segmented square-rounded buttons with clear availability striking, bold sizing labels, and immediate high-contrast obsidian fills upon user selection.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Mechanics:** 20px custom squares (`rounded-sm`) or discs with a 1.5px `#CBD5E1` border.
- **Checked State:** Instant fill with `#FF5500` displaying a crisp white micro-check, eliminating muddy multi-color accents.

### Equipment Spec Sheets & Accordions
- **Technical Specs:** Modular key-value pairs alternating between `#FFFFFF` and `#F8F9FA` rows, bordered by `#EDF2F7`, utilizing `body-sm` and `label-caps` for swift athletic metric scannability.
